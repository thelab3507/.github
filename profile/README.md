# The Lab

**Reps in the lab: builds and contributions sparked by the news.**

The Lab is a build series. Every couple of weeks I pick something that stands out in tech news (a new tool, model, API or open dataset) and ship a small public project around it: a tool, an app, a library or a contribution to an existing project, plus a short writeup.

## How it works

1. **Spot it.** A topic scout reads tech feeds and flags items that spark something worth building.
2. **Build it.** About two weeks per project, scoped small enough to finish.
3. **Ship it.** Public repo, a working demo where possible, and a writeup of what worked and what didn't.
